# AutoSEO (getautoseo.com) — Product Teardown

**Workstream A** · Research date: July 2026 · Method: public site analysis, signup flow, example articles, third-party reviews

---

## Signup & onboarding

| Step | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| Entry | Homepage URL field → "Get 3 Articles + 30-Day Content Plan" |
| Signup | `/signup` — "Takes 4 minutes · No credit card needed" |
| Promise | 30 articles/mo, 100 DA backlinks, full autopilot |
| Friction | Minimal — website URL is the primary input |

**Time-to-value target:** under 15 minutes from URL to first draft visible.

## Four-step growth engine (marketing)

1. **Deep research** — AI studies business, customers, competitors; analyzes 500+ competitor terms; uses GSC when connected
2. **Daily articles** — 1 expert article/day, 1,500–2,500 words, problem-solving angles
3. **Backlinks** — 5–10/month from vetted customer exchange network; "100 DA worth/month"
4. **Traffic** — claims 50–140% organic traffic in 90 days; AI recommendation visibility

## Article quality scorecard (sample articles, Jul 2026)

Scored against goals.ac rubric (structure, voice, citations, YMYL, edit burden).

| Sample | Words | Structure | Voice | Citations | YMYL | Edit burden |
|--------|-------|-----------|-------|-----------|------|-------------|
| Peptide storage (health) | 2,986 | Strong H2/H3, FAQ, tables | Professional, niche-specific | NIBSC external link | YMYL disclaimer present | Low–medium |
| Financial forecast (startup) | 2,837 | Step-by-step, key takeaways | Founder-friendly | Generic | N/A | Low |

**Hypothesis result:** Quality is **good enough for SMB publish-with-minor-edits** — validates goals.ac humanization + editorial control as differentiation, not table-stakes quality gap.

## Dashboard & transparency (claimed)

- See every article before publish
- Track every backlink built
- Real-time dashboard updates
- Edit or request changes on articles

**Parity gap for goals.ac:** unified autopilot activity view (articles + visibility; no backlink tracker by design).

## Friction notes (A5)

- Cancel: 1-click, money-back (7-day monthly, 30-day annual)
- Support: in-app chat (Trustpilot mentions)
- Billing: transparent monthly; some reviews cite $99/mo as expensive for slow SEO results
