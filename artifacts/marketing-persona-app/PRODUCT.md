# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** B2B startup founders and growth leads who need SEO, GEO, and content production without hiring a full agency first. They work in focused sessions — researching keywords, approving drafts, publishing to their CMS, checking visibility — often switching between laptop and mobile.

**Secondary:** Agencies and consultants managing content programs for multiple client workspaces. They need trustworthy multi-project navigation, editorial review gates, and proof they can show clients (audits, calendars, published URLs).

Marketing pages speak to evaluators who may not be daily product users yet; the in-app experience serves people already doing the work.

## Product Purpose

goals.ac is an AI-powered programmatic SEO and content growth platform. It turns brand context, keyword research, and competitive signals into tailored roadmaps, brief-driven drafts, GEO audits, and cross-platform CMS publishing — with human review before anything goes live.

Success means founders and agencies ship more high-quality, citation-ready content faster, with measurable movement in search and AI visibility, without the interface feeling like another generic AI dashboard.

## Positioning

The growth roadmap and content studio that already knows your brand, CMS, and competitive landscape — so every draft is publish-ready strategy, not blank-page AI slop.

## Brand Personality

Premium minimal — quiet luxury through restraint, not decoration. Understated confidence: lots of intentional whitespace, calm forest-and-paper palette, serif accent only where editorial voice earns it. Copy is direct and expert, never hypey or urgency-stacked. The tool should feel like it disappears behind the work.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI SaaS: purple gradients, Inter/default grotesque, hero-metric stat grids, identical icon+heading+blurb feature cards, left accent rails on list rows
- SEO-tool spam: neon urgency badges, keyword-stuffed layouts, aggressive countdown patterns, "powered by AI" section scaffolding
- Training-data editorial clichés: cream/sand body backgrounds as the entire personality, tiny tracked eyebrows on every section, numbered 01/02/03 section markers without a real sequence

## Design Principles

1. **Dual register routing:** `(public)` marketing routes use **brand** register — editorial restraint, conversion clarity, magazine-quality typography. `(app)` authenticated routes use **product** register — workflow-first, density only where it speeds the job.
2. **Premium minimal:** Whitespace and hierarchy do the work; decoration is earned, never default.
3. **Practice what you preach:** The product UI should demonstrate the content quality and SEO craft goals.ac sells — structured, readable, citation-ready, not dashboard slop.
4. **Self-serve → program escalation:** Free/signup path stays frictionless; pricing and contact surfaces explain when hands-on programs make sense without blocking exploration.
5. **Proof over promises:** Prefer real outputs (draft previews, audit findings, roadmap artifacts) over abstract feature claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AAA where feasible**; AA minimum on all interactive surfaces
- Keyboard-operable navigation, dialogs, and data tables; visible focus rings; dialog escape and focus return
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for scroll reveals, hero motion, and non-essential blur
- Body text contrast ≥4.5:1; large text ≥3:1; no washed-out muted gray on tinted backgrounds
- Screen reader labels on icon-only controls; live regions for streaming generation and async job status
